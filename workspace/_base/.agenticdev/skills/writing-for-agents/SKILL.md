# Writing for agents

Write ordered actions with checkable completion criteria. Keep one source of truth, put branch-specific detail behind a precise context pointer, and group a concept with its rules and caveats. Prefer positive target behavior over long prohibition lists. Remove facts the environment can cheaply discover and prune instructions that do not alter behavior.
